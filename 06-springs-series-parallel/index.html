How to use this simulation

  1. Choose the arrangement (A alone, series or parallel) and the two springs.
  2. Add a load with +. Read the total extension x and press ● Record.
  3. Repeat for several loads and arrangements. The Data & analysis tab fits a line for each arrangement: its gradient is the effective spring constant keff.

The physics

  • Series (one below the other): each spring carries the whole load, so the extensions add: x = F/kA + F/kB ⇒ 1/keff = 1/kA + 1/kB.
  • Parallel (side by side, bar kept level): both springs have the same extension and share the load: F = kAx + kBx ⇒ keff = kA + kB.
  • A rubber band does not obey Hooke's law: its F–x graph curves, so it has no single k.

Series and parallel springs are not in the IB SL guide; they extend Hooke's law and match the "car suspension" problem (four springs share the car's weight). Model: light springs and bar, below the elastic limit.
