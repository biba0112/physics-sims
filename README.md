# Physics Simulations — IBDP Physics SL, Grade 11

Interactive simulations for Topic A.2 (forces). They open in any modern browser on laptops, Chromebooks and tablets,
and keep working without internet once loaded.

**All simulations:** https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/

| No. | Simulation | Topic | What students do | Worksheet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Friction block](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/01-friction-block/) | A.2 Types of forces | Static and dynamic friction: pull a block with a newton meter, find μs and μd from a graph. | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/01-friction-block/worksheet.pdf) |
| 02 | [Normal force](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/02-normal-force/) | A.2 Types of forces | What does a scale really measure? Push and pull, a tilted board, a lift and a stack of blocks. | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/02-normal-force/worksheet.pdf) |
| 03 | [Friction on an incline](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/03-friction-incline/) | A.2 Types of forces | Tilt a ramp until the block slips (μs = tan θ), then let it slide and find μd. | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/03-friction-incline/worksheet.pdf) |
| 04 | [Incline with a pulley](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/04-incline-pulley/) | A.2 Forces · Newton's 2nd law | A block on a ramp tied over a pulley to a hanging mass: tension, friction and acceleration. | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/04-incline-pulley/worksheet.pdf) |
| 05 | [Spring force](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/05-spring-force/) | A.2 Types of forces | Hooke's law: hang masses, read the extension, find k ± Δk, and see the elastic limit. | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/05-spring-force/worksheet.pdf) |
| 06 | [Springs in series and parallel](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/06-springs-series-parallel/) | A.2 Types of forces (extension) | Combine two springs: how does the effective spring constant change? | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/06-springs-series-parallel/worksheet.pdf) |
| 07 | [Drag and Stokes' law](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/07-drag-stokes/) | A.2 Types of forces | Ball bearings falling through glycerol: terminal velocity and Stokes' law — plus a skydiver. | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/07-drag-stokes/worksheet.pdf) |
| 08 | [Buoyancy](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/08-buoyancy/) | A.2 Types of forces | Archimedes' principle with a newton meter and a eureka can: sink, float or hover? | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/08-buoyancy/worksheet.pdf) |
| 09 | [Pendulum tension](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/09-pendulum-tension/) | A.2 Forces · circular motion · energy | Hold a pendulum aside or let it swing: how does the tension in the string change? | [PDF](https://biba0112.github.io/physics-sims/09-pendulum-tension/worksheet.pdf) |

Each simulation has a **Tasks** tab with three parts — *Pre-lab* (predict), *During the lab* (compare with the hands-on
activity) and *Homework* (data, graphs and calculations) — matching its worksheet.
