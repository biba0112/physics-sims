How to use this simulation

  1. Choose the object and its size, the string length and the angle θ₀.
  2. ✋ Hold aside: a hand holds the bob still at θ₀ with a horizontal pull. Read the newton meter.
  3. ▶ Release: the bob swings. The newton meter reading changes all the time; the Graph tab shows it. After a few swings press ● Record (peak tension and period).

The physics

  • Length to the centre of the bob: L = string + r.
  • Held aside (equilibrium): T cos θ = mg and T sin θ = Fhand ⇒ T = mg / cos θ.
  • Swinging: along the string the bob moves in a circle, so T − mg cos θ = mv²/L ⇒ T = mg cos θ + mv²/L.
  • At the bottom (energy: v² = 2gL(1 − cos θ₀)): T = mg(3 − 2 cos θ₀) — independent of L!
  • Period for small swings: Tperiod = 2π√(L/g) (topic C.1 preview) — independent of m.

Model: air drag ½ρCdAv² (ρ = 1.2 kg m⁻³, Cd = 0.47) slows large light objects; it acts along the path, so it does not change T directly. The balloon's weight is its rubber (the air inside floats), but the air inside still has to be moved, which makes it sluggish. Light, inextensible string.
